ABOUT MELODY MAGIC DIGITAL AUDITORIUM

Melody Magic Digital Auditorium is an online platform created to bring singers, performers, music lovers and hosts together in one virtual auditorium. Our vision is to make music accessible to everyone, everywhere, without any language, regional or cultural limitations. Whether you sing in Hindi, Marathi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi or any other language, you are welcome here. Performers can discover singing shows, register and showcase their talent. Audiences can join available events, enjoy performances, interact through reactions and support their favourite artists. Approved hosts can create and manage their own digital shows, connect with performers and build their musical communities. From Bollywood retro, regional music and karaoke to classical, devotional, independent music, folk, fusion and open-mic sessions, every genre and every voice has a place here. No language barriers. No boundaries. Just music, talent and togetherness. Join us to share your talent, enjoy music and connect with a growing global musical community. Every voice deserves a stage!

🎤 For Performers

🎧 For Audiences

🎙️ For Hosts

Apply for host access and, once approved, create and manage your own digital shows for performers and audiences.

Build an inclusive musical community without language or regional boundaries, where everyone can share their passion for music.
